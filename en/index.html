01 / ABOUT

Where management meets engineering.

I explore how technology can be understood, put into practice and made useful in real settings.

I hold a Ph.D. in Business Administration from National Central University. My teaching and systems work connect AI, AIoT, cloud and edge computing, with an emphasis on linking concepts, hands-on practice and problem-solving.

From early interactive-device work with ePuppy and eBuddy to AI platforms and interdisciplinary research, I turn questions into clear hypotheses, working prototypes and evidence that can be discussed and reviewed.
